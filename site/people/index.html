Core Faculty

Zac Irving
Director
Philosophy
College of Arts & Sciences

Zac Irving is an Associate Professor of Philosophy and the director of HooCS. Zac's work develops an action-theoretic, scientific, and ethical theory of attention that makes room for spontaneous forms of attention such as mind-wandering. He uses methods from both analytic philosophy and scientific psychology, grounding his scientific theorizing and methods in philosophical foundations.

Jenn MacCormack
Core Faculty
Psychology
College of Arts & Sciences

Dr. Jennifer MacCormack is an Assistant Professor of Social Psychology at the University of Virginia and directs the Virginia Affect and Interoception Laboratory. Broadly, she studies how the brain represents the body's internal states through interoception — and how those representations contribute, consciously and unconsciously, to emotion and social behavior across the lifespan.

Adrienne Wood
Affiliated
Psychology
College of Arts & Sciences

Associate Professor in the Psychology Department. My Emotion and Behavior Lab studies how laughter and conversation help people connect despite their differences, how strangers become friends, and the strategies people use to become well-connected to their communities. We use a variety of methods, including computer vision and acoustic analysis to measure nonverbal behavior, topic modeling, social network analysis, digital interventions, and experience sampling.
